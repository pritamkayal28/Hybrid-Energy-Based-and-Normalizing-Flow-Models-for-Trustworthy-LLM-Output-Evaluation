Large Language Models (LLMs) generate fluent text but often suffer from hallucinations, inconsistency, and
lack of reliability. This project proposes a hybrid framework combining Energy-Based Models (EBMs) and
Normalizing Flow Models (NFMs) to evaluate the trustworthiness of LLM-generated outputs. EBMs are
used to assign unnormalized confidence scores to detect anomalous or low-quality responses, while Normal
izing Flows provide exact likelihood estimation for uncertainty quantification. The hybrid approach enables
both interpretability and probabilistic evaluation of outputs. Experiments are conducted on LLM-generated
responses across factual and reasoning tasks. The proposed system improves robustness, uncertainty aware
ness, and reliability in LLM evaluation.
